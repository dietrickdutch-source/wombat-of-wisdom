# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Wombat Pipeline is a private tool used only by the creator of the Wombat of Wisdom channel to make and schedule his own videos. It is not offered to the public.

## What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool can use these Google services on the owner's own account only:

- Google Sheets and Google Drive: to read and update the channel's own content tracking sheet and upload finished videos and images to the owner's folders.
- YouTube: to upload and schedule videos on the owner's channel and read that channel's view statistics.
- Gmail: to send the owner a short summary email about each run, addressed only to the owner.

## What it stores

Sign-in tokens and working files are stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Contact

Questions can be raised through this site's GitHub repository.
