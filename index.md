# Wombat of Wisdom

Short animated explainers about money, statistics and economics, hosted by a round cartoon wombat with glasses and a green scarf.

Find the channel on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook as @wisdomwombat.

This site is also the home page for the private Wombat Pipeline tool the channel's creator uses to make and schedule videos.
